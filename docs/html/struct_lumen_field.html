Detailed Description

Dense voxel model of the airway lumen together with a distance-to-wall field.

Routing runs on these voxels, so a route cannot leave the airway, and the distance field is what holds it on the lumen axis.

Clicks land on the airway wall, and the camera can drift off the axis, so take the most open voxel within a short reach of the nearest lumen voxel.

Widening the reach further would let a click on a small branch snap into the parent airway.
